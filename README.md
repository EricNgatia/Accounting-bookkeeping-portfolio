# Accounting & Bookkeeping Portfolio

A demonstration project showing practical bookkeeping workflows using **fictional sample data only**. The workbook structure is inspired by common small-business finance tasks: sales recording, collections tracking, expense categorization, receivables monitoring, and monthly management reporting.

> **Privacy note:** This project was recreated from scratch with fictional names, references, dates, and amounts. It does not include the original employer workbook or real company data. Never upload confidential employer records, customer details, payroll data, bank statements, credentials, or proprietary templates to a public repository.

## Portfolio highlights

- **Accounts receivable:** invoice-level balances and collection matching.
- **Cash receipts:** tracking receipt references, payment channels, and customer payments.
- **Expense register:** vendor, category, payment method, and expense review.
- **Receivables aging:** outstanding invoice balances and due dates.
- **Profit & loss:** monthly revenue, cost of sales, operating expenses, profit, and margins.
- **Excel reporting:** linked sheets, formulas, filters, and a management dashboard.
- **Python reporting:** a repeatable pandas workflow that creates a monthly P&L CSV.

## How to explore

1. Open `accounting_bookkeeping_portfolio.xlsx` in Microsoft Excel or LibreOffice Calc.
2. Review the `Dashboard` sheet first.
3. Inspect `Sales`, `Collections`, and `Expenses` for fictional source entries.
4. Review `Receivables` for invoice balances.
5. Review `Profit & Loss` for monthly reporting.
6. See `SETUP.md` to regenerate the CSV report using Python.
